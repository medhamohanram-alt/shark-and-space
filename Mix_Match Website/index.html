Sharks and Space Exploration

Sharks

Believe it or not, NASA technology actually helps protect sharks. Scientists use the same mapping software designed for the Hubble Space Telescope to track whale sharks; since their spots look like stars, the computer can identify individual sharks just like it identifies constellations. Satellites also help researchers monitor the health of the oceans from way up in orbit. It’s a cool example of how exploring the stars can help us save the ancient "aliens" of our own deep seas.
